Contact support

Find the event you want to attend, select your ticket category and quantity, then continue through checkout. Keep your email address and payment method ready before starting your booking.

After successful payment, your order confirmation and ticket options are sent to the email address used during checkout. Digital tickets can also be accessed from your account where available.

For eligible tickets, open your ticket confirmation and use the Transfer Tickets option. Enter the recipient's email address and follow the confirmation steps.

If a ticket category can still be selected and added to your cart, tickets are available for purchase. Availability can change quickly.

Ticket requirements depend on the event. For football matches, every attendee requires a valid ticket, including children under six. Always check the individual event information.

OpenTickets currently provides ticket access through the website and digital delivery channels rather than a dedicated mobile app.
